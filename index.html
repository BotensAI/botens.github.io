BOTENS ROBOTICS

Key Features · Key Technologies

Hardware Layer

Intelligent robot chassis, D1 high-payload platform, lightweight platform and multi-scenario upper-structure solutions. Modular design, customizable by scenario.

Autonomous Driving Layer

Integrated indoor-outdoor autonomous driving, supporting continuous localization, perception, planning and control in unstructured indoor and outdoor environments.

Access Layer

AIoT access-control and elevator integration, low-cost elevator control, access-control AIoT and flexible door openers, solving the robot access bottlenecks of “passing through doors, gates and elevators”.

Operations Layer

Dispatch platform + localized operations, including route planning and dispatch, status monitoring, exception handling and data closed-loop management, supported by local delivery and continuous operating capabilities.

BOTENS ROBOTICS

Core Capabilities

High Payload

Recommended payload ≤50 kg, maximum static payload 200 kg, supporting large-item and multi-stop one-to-many delivery.

Long Endurance

LiFePO4 battery, 12-15 hours of endurance, supports both charging and battery swapping, with a full charge in approximately 3 hours.

Strong Mobility

Six-wheel chassis, gradeability ≤30°, obstacle clearance <3 cm, supporting continuous indoor-outdoor operation.

High Intelligence

Equipped with integrated indoor-outdoor autonomous driving, enabling autonomous localization and navigation, dynamic obstacle avoidance and route planning.

Full Perception

Multi-sensor fusion of 3D LiDAR, wide-angle waterproof cameras, 3D depth cameras and ultrasonic radar to accurately identify pedestrians, vehicles and obstacles.

Customizable

Body height and upper structure can be customized by scenario, using a modular platform to quickly adapt to different industries.

Unmanned Operation

Together with the dispatch platform and AIoT access infrastructure, the robot can support 24-hour unmanned operation and remote O&M.

Smart Delivery Scenarios

Community Delivery
